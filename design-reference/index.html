Why join

No experience Coaching starts from zero

Half of every intake has never picked up a stick. Sessions are built around whoever shows up, not who's played before.

BUCS + socials A season that's more than fixtures

Three sides competing across BUCS London, plus the socials that end up being everyone's favourite part of first year.

Community Your people in a very big med school

Imperial's medical school is huge. This is the fastest way to find a group that sticks with you through all six years.

Not just medics Open to the whole of Imperial

You don't need to be studying medicine and you don't need to have ever picked up a stick — trials are open to anyone who wants in.

Back-to-back Varsity champions, two years running

The squad's turned Varsity into a habit. Come find out what the noise on the coach home is about.

2+ tours a season Mixed socials, not just a boys' club

Joint socials and an end-of-season tour every year, sometimes two — the calendar's about a lot more than training.
